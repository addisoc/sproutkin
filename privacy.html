Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Short version: Sproutkin stores everything you log locally on your device. We don't have accounts or servers, we don't collect or transmit your data, and we don't track you or use third-party analytics or advertising.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Sproutkin has no user accounts and no backend servers. All information you enter — such as your baby's name, birthdate, photo, logged activities, milestones, and reminders — is stored only on your device.

How your data is stored

Your data is saved locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party. If you create a backup or export within the app, that file is created on your device and it is your responsibility to store it securely.

Photos

If you add a photo of your baby, it is stored locally on your device and used only within the app. It is never uploaded or shared by the app.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Sproutkin schedules local notifications on your device. These are handled entirely by iOS on your device and are not sent through any external service.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies. Sproutkin does not track you across apps or websites, and its privacy manifest declares no data collection and no tracking.

Third parties

Sproutkin does not share your information with third parties because it does not collect or transmit your information in the first place.

Children's privacy

Sproutkin is intended for use by parents and caregivers to record information about their child. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no personal information about a child is ever sent to us or to third parties.

Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you can remove it at any time by deleting your entries within the app or by deleting the app, which removes all associated data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.
